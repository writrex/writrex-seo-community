# Frequently asked questions

## What is Writrex SEO?

Writrex SEO is an AI Website & SEO Auditor. It examines a public webpage and returns structured, evidence-based on-page SEO information to help you understand what is present and what may need investigation.

## What can an audit measure?

Depending on the public page, it can inspect HTTP response and indexability signals, page title, meta description, canonical and robots directives, headings, main-content word count, links, images and alt attributes, structured data, and evidence-based observations.

A result reflects the page accessible at that time. It is not a guarantee of search ranking, indexing or business performance.

## Can it access private pages?

No. Writrex SEO is designed for public webpages. Never submit login details, private URLs or sensitive information.

## Does it change my website?

No. Audits are read-only. Writrex SEO does not edit your website, publish content or change settings.

## Why can results differ between audits?

Webpages can change, respond differently by time or location, block automated requests, or rely on browser-only content. Treat results as evidence for investigation.

## How do I report a problem?

Use the [issue forms](../../issues/new/choose). Include the public URL where relevant, approximate time, expected result and actual result. Never include credentials, payment data or private reports.

## How do I report a security concern?

Do not create a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Where can I see availability?

Visit [Writrex SEO system status](https://seo.writrex.com/status). Recorded history clearly distinguishes monitored and unmonitored periods.
