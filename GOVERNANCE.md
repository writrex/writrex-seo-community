# Community governance

Writrex maintains this repository as a public feedback and documentation space.

Maintainers review reports, keep information accurate and protect the community from unsafe or confidential content. Product decisions are informed by customer impact, reliability, security, evidence quality, accessibility and long-term maintainability.

Not every accepted idea will be scheduled. Roadmap items can change. Maintainers may label, edit, close, convert, lock or remove content to keep the repository constructive and safe.

We aim to explain significant public roadmap changes without publishing private customer information, security-sensitive details, commercial negotiations or infrastructure information.
