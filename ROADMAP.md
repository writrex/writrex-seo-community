# Public roadmap

This is the direction of Writrex SEO, not a delivery contract or fixed schedule. Priorities can change based on customer impact, reliability, security and product learning.

## Now

- Improve clarity and usefulness of evidence-based audit reports
- Strengthen account, credit and purchase visibility
- Improve product reliability and public status transparency
- Make measured findings, opportunities and untested areas easier to understand

## Next

- More practical implementation guidance for common technical SEO findings
- Improved report organisation and sharing workflows
- Better comparison and repeat-analysis experience
- Continued accessibility and mobile usability improvements

## Exploring

- Search performance and Search Console-informed insights
- Content decay and content-priority signals
- Internal-linking opportunities
- Keyword cannibalisation discovery
- Local SEO planning workflows
- Multi-site and agency-oriented management

## Released

- Evidence-based on-page audits for public webpages
- Account and credit workflows
- Technical and comprehensive audit-report foundations
- Public status page with recorded platform-check history

## Influence the roadmap

Open a feature request or discussion with the problem, who it affects, your current workaround and what success would look like. We prioritise practical customer impact over raw feature count.
