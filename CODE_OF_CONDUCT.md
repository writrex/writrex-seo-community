# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Writrex SEO Community should be constructive, respectful and useful for people with different backgrounds, experience levels and viewpoints.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and professional
- Focus on evidence, clear examples and practical outcomes
- Respect privacy and confidentiality
- Give feedback about ideas and behaviour, not personal attacks
- Keep discussion relevant to Writrex SEO

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks
- Publishing private, confidential or identifying information
- Posting credentials, payment details, access tokens or security exploits
- Spam, deceptive promotion or repeated off-topic content
- Attempts to bypass access controls or misuse the service

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, lock or remove content and may restrict participation where necessary to keep the community safe and useful.

For a conduct concern, contact [hello@writrex.com](mailto:hello@writrex.com) with the subject `Community conduct concern`. Do not publicly name people while a concern is being reviewed.
